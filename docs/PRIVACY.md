# NixPlayer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026
Publisher: NixNasim (Nasimul Islam Nasim)

NixPlayer is a desktop video and audio player. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking. This page explains what stays on your computer and what is sent over the internet, and only when you use the matching feature.

## What stays on your computer
- Your media files are played locally. NixPlayer never uploads them.
- Settings, watch history, resume positions, window size and saved subtitles are stored in NixPlayer's own app-data folder on your PC. They are never sent to the publisher.
- Uninstalling NixPlayer removes the app; you can delete the app-data folder to remove the settings.

## What is sent over the internet (only when you use the feature)
- **Subtitle search (OpenSubtitles):** when you search for subtitles, NixPlayer sends the OpenSubtitles service a search text (for example the file name), the chosen language and, for a file match, the file size and a short fingerprint (a hash) of the file. It does not send the video itself.
- **Translate a subtitle line or word:** the selected text is sent to translation services (Google Translate and MyMemory) to get the translation.
- **Dictionary lookup:** the selected word is sent to dictionary services (dictionaryapi.dev and Wiktionary).
- **Open link:** when you open a web link, NixPlayer connects to that address to play it. The server you open can see your IP address, as with any browser.

These services have their own privacy policies. NixPlayer does not send them your name, email or any account information, because NixPlayer does not have any.

## What NixPlayer does not do
- No tracking, advertising or analytics.
- No collection or sale of personal data.
- No access to your camera or microphone.

## Children
NixPlayer is a general-purpose media player and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
